## 3. Confidence semantic separation

Confidence means reliability, not pair attractiveness. A future cutoff may consume only a separately approved overall Confidence Score representation applicable to Qualification. It cannot silently consume or derive from:

- Architecture `evidence_status`;
- feature/value Evidence Confidence or `required_evidence_level`;
- model probability, parser/validation confidence or AI self-confidence;
- Match Score, Risk Score, completeness, ranking position or Priority Score;
- `unknown`, `abstention`, review flag, Qualification result or user-facing wording.

No ordering, numeric mapping, bucket, band, severity, status alias or fallback across these spaces is approved.

## 4. Calibration prerequisite, not cutoff

`XFR-D-063` Confidence calibration evidence must be available and compatible before any future cutoff approval. Calibration target, goodness-of-fit result, score distribution or conventional operating point does not determine the Qualification cutoff automatically. Calibration definition/method/result and cutoff comparator/value remain separate OPEN decisions.

`XFR-D-045` and open `XFR-F1` remain mandatory independent prerequisites. A future evidence package requires baseline-first measurement, an immutable frozen manifest, tuning/untouched-final isolation, eligible labels and adjudication, grouping/allocation/correction provenance, uncertainty/calibration reporting, segment/intersection/fairness/legal review, false-eligibility and false-exclusion counter-evidence, and synthetic/production separation.

## 5. Qualification-only authority and non-compensation

A future separately approved cutoff may govern only affected Qualification progression. It cannot recalculate or redefine Match Score, Dimension Score, Priority Score, Risk, ranking, Hard Constraint results or evidence status.

Mutual fit, Confidence and completeness remain separate non-compensating families. Low Risk, ranking quality, aggregate performance, business outcome or high score cannot compensate for insufficient Confidence evidence. Confidence cannot compensate for failure in those independent families. Synthetic-only evidence cannot create a production cutoff, production-readiness claim or runtime permission.

## 6. No-surrogate, no-default and fail-closed boundary

No model probability, midpoint, scale endpoint, percentile, observed distribution, prior-version cutoff, calibration target, pilot KPI, Campaign conversion target, business preference, UI band or implementation default may serve as a cutoff surrogate. No comparator, numeric value, direction or equality treatment is selected here.

Missing, unapproved, unmapped, ambiguous, stale, conflicting, incomplete or version-incompatible Confidence definition, calibration, cutoff, evidence or policy binding fails closed only for affected Qualification progression. It creates no negative fact, zero confidence, guessed band, Hard Constraint failure, automatic `INELIGIBLE`, `REJECTED_BY_MATCHING`, `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`, `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `QUALIFIED_HYPOTHESIS`, rank change or unrelated processing block.

## 3. Completeness semantic and representation separation

A future completeness object may evaluate only a separately approved critical-data set for a specific Qualification use and purpose. It cannot silently mean generic profile completeness, filled-field percentage, any evidence present, absence of null, acceptable Confidence, dataset coverage or profile readiness.

No numeric threshold, all-required deterministic rule, typed composite rule or other exact representation is selected, preferred, excluded or approved. Critical-data completeness remains distinct from Feature Schema `required_evidence_level`, Architecture `evidence_status`, freshness, dataset-label completeness, allocation completeness, segment coverage and statistical power.

## 4. Evidence, non-compensation and baseline discipline

`XFR-D-045` and open `XFR-F1` remain mandatory independent evidence prerequisites. A future evidence package must separately record candidate set/applicability/use/purpose, source authority and evidence dependencies, candidate definition/counting unit/missing-state treatment, baseline before candidate-rule search, immutable frozen manifest, tuning/untouched-final isolation, eligible provenance, uncertainty and counter-evidence, segment/intersection/legal/fairness review, synthetic/production separation, version/hash binding and full verdict.

Mutual fit, Confidence and completeness remain separate non-compensating families. High score, low Risk, ranking quality, business urgency or aggregate performance cannot compensate for completeness insufficiency. Aggregates across noncritical fields, rows, users, Campaigns, segments or time windows cannot compensate for a missing approved critical input or required evidence unless separately approved; G47 approves no such aggregation.

## 5. No-surrogate, no-default and fail-closed boundary

A conventional percentage, non-null/all-fields ratio, treating all required fields as critical, `required_evidence_level`, dataset/segment coverage or implementation default cannot serve as the completeness rule.

Missing, unapproved, ambiguous, stale, conflicting, incomplete or incompatible critical-data set, definition, threshold/rule, evidence or policy binding fails closed only for affected Qualification progression. It creates no zero completeness, negative fact, Hard Constraint failure, automatic `INELIGIBLE`, rejection, review route, `QUALIFIED_HYPOTHESIS`, Risk result, rank change or unrelated block.

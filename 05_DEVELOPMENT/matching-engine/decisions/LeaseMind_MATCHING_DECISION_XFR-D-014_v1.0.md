## 3. Решение

### 3.1. Governance roles и разделение authority

1. Governance owner of the final-verdict question for the 20 candidates — `LEGAL`.
2. Mandatory approvers — `Chief AI Architect + PRODUCT + AI + DEVELOPMENT`. No future item-specific verdict for any candidate is approved without this full set.
3. Evidence/technical-procedure owner — `AI + DEVELOPMENT`; this role prepares and verifies candidate evidence, technical procedures and feasibility analysis only and has no unilateral authority to approve a verdict, classification, lawful basis, compatibility or evidence-sufficiency determination, Policy, runtime or implementation.
4. `PRODUCT` retains authority over intended product constraint meaning; `Chief AI Architect` retains architecture and non-conflation oversight; `AI`/`DEVELOPMENT` remain evidence/technical roles. None of them substitutes for `LEGAL` ownership of legal admissibility, protected/proxy/discrimination classification, lawful basis and rights impact.
5. Governance ownership, evidence preparation, data authority, policy approval, sync and gate decision are different authority layers; no layer replaces another.

### 3.2. No final LEGAL verdict; candidate status unchanged

1. This record issues no final LEGAL verdict for any of the 20 candidates. No candidate is approved, classified, rejected, excluded or promoted by this record.
2. All 20 candidates remain `ELIGIBILITY_HARD_CONSTRAINT_CANDIDATE`; `automatic_ineligible_allowed` remains `NO` for all of them.
3. No candidate becomes a confirmed Hard Constraint, and none receives an approved protected/proxy/discrimination classification, lawful basis, compatibility result, required evidence level or automatic `INELIGIBLE` consequence.
4. The record does not declare §14.3 условие 4 (or any other §14.3 condition) satisfied or failed for any candidate.
5. Feature Schema rows, readiness states and all registry/proposal content are unchanged by this record; only a future separate sync may overlay this boundary.

### 3.3. Future item-specific verdict prerequisites

Any future item-specific verdict on any of the 20 candidates requires a separate versioned decision that:

1. binds the exact candidate (`feature_id` and §5.1 registry №), source/bootstrap path and comparison operator, intended use, applicable Feature Schema/candidate version and content hash, plus version/hash-bound source evidence and provenance;
2. separately verifies and records each of: (a) protected/proxy/discrimination classification under §14.3 условие 4; (b) lawful basis; (c) compatibility under the applicable remnants of `XFR-D-001`/`XFR-D-002`/`XFR-D-012`; and (d) evidence sufficiency under the applicable `XFR-D-M1` boundary;
3. passes the full owner/approver set of §3.1 on the same version/hash and consumes the then-applicable approved `XFR-D-054` protected/proxy catalog/lawful-basis boundary and the applicable `XFR-D-064`/`XFR-D-068` Evaluation-domain boundaries where the candidate/use intersects them;
4. receives no authorization from this record by implication.

### 3.4. Confirmed protected/proxy means unconditional exclusion

1. If a future item-specific verdict confirms that a candidate, source field, code point or use is protected, a proxy of a protected attribute, or a discriminatory restriction, the result is unconditional exclusion of that candidate/use — not approval after review, not a mitigated approval, not a conditional Hard Constraint.
2. Lawful basis cannot override that prohibition: a lawful basis for processing does not make a confirmed protected/proxy candidate usable as a Hard Constraint, exclusion ground or eligibility input.
3. This mirrors — and does not extend beyond its own domain — the Architecture §17 prohibition on protected attributes/proxies in Risk Score and the `XFR-D-068` diagnostic-vs-legal non-conflation rule; exact legal standard and classification method remain `OPEN`.

3. An unresolved evidence state does not authorize a guessed verdict, guessed classification, invented fallback, derived route, display text or history mutation.

## 7. Rationale

Feature Schema proposes 20 Hard Constraint candidates from the Technical Assignment bootstrap mapping, but the repository has not approved the Feature Schema, has not approved a per-feature required evidence level, and — critically — has not obtained the LEGAL determination required by §14.3 условие 4. Approving, reclassifying or rejecting candidates now, or letting provisional/technical text harden into a verdict, would turn candidate registry rows and evidence states into exclusion authority they do not have. The narrow governance boundary assigns the question to `LEGAL` with a full approver set, keeps all 20 candidates in their unchanged candidate status with `automatic_ineligible_allowed = NO`, fixes the unconditional-exclusion consequence of a confirmed protected/proxy, and keeps promotion blocked by insufficient evidence without inventing negative facts. This preserves the dependency chains already recorded by `XFR-D-010`, `XFR-D-064`, `XFR-D-068` and `XFR-D-M1` and the sibling boundaries of `XFR-D-004` and `XFR-D-007`.
